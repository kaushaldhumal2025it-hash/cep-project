UPI Safety Rules

The most important rules every senior must follow to never fall prey to phone frauds and scams.

🚨 Golden Rules for UPI Safety (Read Carefully)

  • ⛔
    GOLDEN RULE: Entering your secret UPI PIN sends money OUT of your bank account. You NEVER need to enter your PIN or scan a QR code to receive money!
  • 🔒
    NEVER Share Your PIN or OTP: Bank managers, police, or customer care will NEVER ask for your UPI PIN or SMS OTP over the phone.
  • 🚫
    Beware of Lottery or Pension Links: Never click strange links sent on WhatsApp or SMS claiming you have won money or need to pay electricity bills urgently.
  • 📞
    When in Doubt, Ask Family: If you feel hurried or confused, stop immediately and call your family member or the Cyber Helpline at 1930.

🧘 Gentle daily exercises

Stop if you feel pain, dizziness, or breathlessness. Use a sturdy chair and ask for help.

  1. Chair march: Sit tall and lift one knee at a time for 1 minute.
  2. Ankle circles: Move each ankle slowly 10 times in each direction.
  3. Shoulder rolls: Roll shoulders gently 10 times, breathing slowly.
  4. Supported sit-to-stand: Hold a chair and stand up, then sit down 5 times.
